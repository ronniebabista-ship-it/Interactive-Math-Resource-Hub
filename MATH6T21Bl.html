Mission: How Many Times? 🚀

Given a ratio, find how many times one value is larger than another — and vice versa!

🚀 Use division to find "how many times" from a ratio.
🔄 Turn "times" sentences back into ratios.
🪐 Express the smaller value as a fraction of the larger.
WARM-UP, CADET!

Ratios Compare by Multiplication!

☄️ ☄️ : 🌠 🌠 🌠 🌠 🌠 🌠

2 comets : 6 meteors — the meteors make 3 groups of 2!

6 ÷ 2 = 3meteors = 3 × comets

"For every" and "how many times" are the SAME idea — multiplicative comparison!

KEY IDEA 🔑

HOW MANY TIMES = DIVIDE!

Compared quantity ÷ base quantity = TIMES

6 meteors÷2 comets=3 times

Read the question first: "how many times A is B" means B ÷ A — order decides the division!

EXHIBIT 1 🤖

Astronauts & Robots — 4 Times!

🧑🚀 ASTRONAUTS🤖 ROBOTS 14 28 312

Every row: robots ÷ astronauts = 4 → robots are 4 times the astronauts!

0 1 2 3 0 4 8 12

Each jump of 1 astronaut lines up with a jump of 4 robots — same multiplier every time!

VICE VERSA 🔄

"Times" Sentence → Ratio!

"The 🔭 telescope is 4 times as long as the 🔬 microscope."

telescope : microscope=4 : 1
microscope : telescope=1 : 4

Vice versa FLIPS the ratio — the smaller quantity becomes 1 part of the bigger one's 4!

VICE VERSA 2 🪐

The Fraction Side!

:

Mars : Earth = 1 : 2

Mars = ½ (0.5×) of EarthEarth = 2 × Mars

Smaller ÷ bigger = the FRACTION side • bigger ÷ smaller = the TIMES side!

SITUATION A ☄️

Star Chart — 3 Times!

5 comets : 15 meteors → 15 ÷ 5 = 3 → meteors are 3 times the comets!

comets = 5/15 = 1/3 of meteorsmeteors = 3 × comets

Times and fractions are two sides of the SAME ratio!

SITUATION B 🛢️

Fuel Check — 4 Times!

Small tanks : big tanks = 2 : 8 → 8 ÷ 2 = 4 → a big tank holds 4 times as much!

small = 2/8 = 1/4 of bigbig = 4 × small

Same ratio, two stories — times side and fraction side!

MISSION HAZARDS!

Watch Out, Cadet! 🕳️

Hazard 1 • ÷ not −

6 is 4 MORE than 2, but 3 TIMES 2 — "how many times" means DIVIDE!

Hazard 2 • Order

"A is 4 times B" → A:B = 4:1, NOT 1:4 — the "times" quantity gets the bigger part!

Hazard 3 • Flip = fraction

If big = 4 × small, then small = ¼ of big — vice versa flips ratio AND gives the fraction side!
