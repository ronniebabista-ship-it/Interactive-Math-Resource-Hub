💡 How to Add & Subtract Decimals

🎯 The Golden Rule:
1️⃣ Line up the decimal points vertically (stack them on top of each other).
2️⃣ Add trailing zeros so both numbers have the same number of decimal places.
3️⃣ Add or subtract column by column, starting from the right.
4️⃣ Bring the decimal point straight down into your answer.
📝 Example: Add 3.45 + 2.178
  3.450
+2.178
  5.628
⚠️ Common Mistake:
Don't line up the right ends of the numbers!
❌ Wrong:   3.45
           + 2.178
✅ Right:    3.450
           + 2.178

💡 Tip: Always line up the decimal points!
