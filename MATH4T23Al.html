LAB HAZARDS! ⚠️

Watch Out, Lab Assistant! 🧪

Hazard 1 • Wrong Operation

Big→small MULTIPLIES (3 m = 300 cm). Small→big DIVIDES (300 cm = 3 m). Swap them and your answer sinks!

Hazard 2 • Wrong Magic Number

m→cm uses 100, but km→m, kg→g, g→mg and L→mL all use 1000! Only centimeters use 100!

Hazard 3 • Mixed Units

6 km 500 m = 6500 m — convert 6 km to 6000 m FIRST, then add. Never just glue digits: 650 and 60500 are traps!
