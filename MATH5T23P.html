💡 Solving Multi-Step Problems

🎯 The Golden Rules:
1️⃣ Read carefully — What is the problem asking?
2️⃣ Identify the steps — What comes first? What comes next?
3️⃣ Line up the decimal points when adding or subtracting.
4️⃣ Solve one step at a time — don't rush!
5️⃣ Check your answer — Does it make sense?
📝 Steps for Money Problems:
• Spending? → Subtract from the total.
• Earning/Saving? → Add to the total.
• Finding change? → Total paid − Total spent.
• Finding what's left? → Start − Each expense.
Example: Maria has ₱50. She bought a notebook for ₱18.50 and a pencil for ₱7.25. How much change?

Step 1: Add the costs: ₱18.50 + ₱7.25 = ₱25.75
Step 2: Subtract from her money: ₱50.00 − ₱25.75 = ₱24.25 ✅

💡 Tip: Write it vertically and line up the decimal points!
