Lesson: Multi-Step Decimal Problems

Solve multi-step problems involving addition and/or subtraction of decimals, including money — formally, completely, and checked.

📖 READ & UNDERSTAND: identify the given, the asked, and the hidden question.
🧮 PLAN & SOLVE: sequence the operations with aligned decimal points.
✅ CHECK & ANSWER: estimate, use the inverse operation, and write a complete sentence.
SECTION 1 • DEFINITION

What Is a Multi-Step Problem?

Liza bought a book for ₱245.75 and a pencil case for ₱132.25. She paid the cashier ₱500.00. How much change did she receive?

HIDDEN QUESTION: What is the total cost of the two items?

a multi-step problem needs 2 or more operations — the hidden question is solved first.

GIVEN: the factsHIDDEN: solve firstASKED: the main question

Identifying these three parts correctly is half of the solution.

COMMON ERRORS TO AVOID ⚠️

Three Errors That Void a Solution

Error 1 • Wrong Order

Answering the asked question before the hidden question. The steps must follow their order: hidden first, asked second.

Error 2 • Misaligned Points

Misaligning decimal points in an intermediate computation corrupts every step after it.

Error 3 • Wrong Final Value

Reporting the intermediate result (₱193.25) as the answer, or omitting the unit and the complete sentence.

Discipline in procedure is what makes a solution correct.
