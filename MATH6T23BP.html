💡 How Percent is Used in Real Life

🏷️ DISCOUNT Shows how much price is reduced during sales. "50% OFF on all shoes!"
🏦 INTEREST Shows money earned (savings) or owed (loans). "3% interest per year in the bank."
🧾 TAX Government charge added to purchases or income. "12% VAT included in the price."
💼 COMMISSION Pay earned as a percent of sales. "Agent gets 5% of every sale."
🍽️ SERVICE CHARGE Extra fee for service at restaurants/hotels. "10% service charge added to bill."
📊 STATISTICS Shows survey results or parts of a group. "85% of students passed."
📱 PROGRESS Shows how much of something is complete. "Phone battery at 35%."
🎲 PROBABILITY Shows the chance an event will happen. "70% chance of rain tomorrow."
🍎 NUTRITION Shows daily value of nutrients in food. "25% of daily Vitamin C."
📚 GRADES Shows a test score out of 100. "Maria scored 92% on the test."
🤔 Why use percent?
• Makes comparison easy (50% vs 25% is clearer than 25/50 vs 1/4)
• Standardizes things to a common base of 100
• Works for any size of number (₱10 or ₱1,000,000 — the percent is still meaningful)
🔍 Quick Check — Which use is it?
• Price reduction? → Discount 🏷️
• Money growing/shrinking in bank? → Interest 🏦
• Government fee on receipt? → Tax 🧾
• Pay based on sales made? → Commission 💼
• Extra restaurant fee? → Service charge 🍽️
• Survey, poll, or group results? → Statistics 📊
• Loading bar or battery? → Progress 📱
• Chance of something happening? → Probability 🎲
• Food package label? → Nutrition 🍎
• Test or exam score? → Grade 📚
