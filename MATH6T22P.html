💡 Solving Ratio & Proportion

🎯 What is a Ratio?
A ratio compares two quantities. Example: 3 boys : 4 girls

🎯 What is a Proportion?
A proportion says two ratios are equal. Example: 3:4 = 12:x
📝 Steps to Solve:
1️⃣ Set up the proportion: a/b = c/x
2️⃣ Cross multiply: a × x = b × c
3️⃣ Solve for x: x = (b × c) ÷ a
4️⃣ Check your answer!
3 / 4 = 12 / ?
Example: At Aling Nena's sari-sari store, 3 candies cost ₱6. How much do 5 candies cost?

Set up: 3/6 = 5/x
Cross multiply: 3 × x = 6 × 5
Solve: 3x = 30 → x = 30 ÷ 3 = ₱10 ✅

💡 Tip: If 3 candies cost ₱6, then 1 candy costs ₱2. So 5 candies = 5 × ₱2 = ₱10 ✅
