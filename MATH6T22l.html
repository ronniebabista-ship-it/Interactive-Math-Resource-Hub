12 Mangoes, 18 Bananas — What's the Ratio?

🥭 mangoes 🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭🥭
🍌 bananas 🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌🍌

mangoes : bananas = 12 : 18 → divide both by 6 → 2 : 3!

ratio = compare by DIVISIONnot by subtraction!always simplify — sulit!
UNIT 1 🥭

Ratio Language — Three Ways to Say It!

12 : 18 12 to 18 12⁄18 ➜ ÷6 ➜ 2 : 3

ORDER matters: "mangoes to bananas" puts mangoes FIRST — flip it and the ratio changes!

divide both terms by the GCF12 & 18 share 6 → 2 : 3serve ratios in simplest form!

Proportion Check — Cross-Products!

2 : 3 = 4 : 6 2 × 6 = 12 3 × 4 = 12

cross-products EQUAL → true proportion! multiply DIAGONALLY, never straight across!

a : b = c : d→ a×d and b×cequal? proportion!

SHOP HAZARDS! ⚠️

Ayusin Muna, Ka-Shop! 🕳️

Hazard 1 • Wrong Order

Boys : girls is NOT girls : boys! Match the exact order of the question or the whole proportion flips.

Hazard 2 • Adding Trap

2 : 3 does NOT become 4 : 5 by adding 2! Ratios grow by MULTIPLYING both terms — 2 : 3 = 4 : 6.

Hazard 3 • Straight-Across

Cross-products are DIAGONAL: in a⁄b = c⁄d multiply a×d and b×c — never the tops together!
