KEY IDEA

What is a Number Sentence?

A math sentence with numbers, an operation symbol, and an equal sign!

8 + 5 = 13

Answer not known yet? Use an unknown n (or ?)!

8+n =13

The n is the missing price tag we must find! 🏷️

SITUATION 1 • ADDITION

Sardines In All! 🥫

"Aling Nena sold 24 cans of sardines in the morning and 18 more in the afternoon. How many cans IN ALL?"

🔑 Keyword: IN ALL → ADDITION! Two parts join into one total.

🥫 24+🥫 18
24 + 18 = n

n = 42 cans — but our job is to WRITE the sentence! ✔

SITUATION 2 • SUBTRACTION

Eggs Left! 🥚

"The store had 60 eggs. A baker bought 25. How many are LEFT?"

🔑 Keyword: LEFT → SUBTRACTION! Start with the WHOLE, take away the part.

60 − 25 = n

Watch 25 eggs fade as SOLD 🧺 → n = 35 left!

SITUATION 3 • MULTIPLICATION

Candy Packs — Equal Groups! 🍬

"Aling Nena hangs 4 packs of candy. EACH pack has 6 candies. How many in all?"

🔑 Keywords: EACH + equal groups → MULTIPLICATION! It's fast repeated addition.

4 × 6 = n

6+6+6+6 is the same as 4 × 6 → n = 24! ✔

SITUATION 4 • DIVISION

Fair Sharing — Divide! 🧺

"30 candies are SHARED EQUALLY among 5 children. How many does each child get?"

🔑 Keyword: SHARED EQUALLY → DIVISION! The total is dealt into equal groups.

30 ÷ 5 = n

Each basket ends with 6 → n = 6! Fair! ✔

BOSS LEVEL 👑 TWO OPERATIONS

Miguel's Sukli! 💰

"Miguel buys 3 notebooks at ₱15 EACH and pays ₱50. How much is his CHANGE?"

🔑 "EACH" → multiply first. "CHANGE" → subtract. TWO operations in ONE sentence!

50 − ( 3 × 15 ) = n

( ) keeps the multiplication together FIRST → 50 − 45 = ₱5 sukli!

WATCH OUT!

Store Hazards! 🕳️

Hazard 1 • Wrong operation

"how many MORE" ≠ add

it means SUBTRACT ✔

Comparing = difference!

Hazard 2 • Wrong order

25 − 60 ✗

60 − 25 ✔

Whole first in − and ÷!

Hazard 3 • Missing pieces

60 − 25 ✗

60 − 25 = n ✔

Always include = and the unknown!
