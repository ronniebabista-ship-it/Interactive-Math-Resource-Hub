📝 How to play:
Get your paper and pencil ready!
Practice rounding decimal numbers to the nearest thousandths!
💡 Rounding Rules:
🔹 The thousandths place is the 3rd digit after the decimal.
🔹 The ten-thousandths place is the 4th digit after the decimal.
🔹 5 or more? Raise the score! (Round up the 3rd digit)
🔹 4 or less? Let it rest! (Keep the 3rd digit the same)
🔹 Example: 3.14159 → Look at 5 → Round up → 3.142
